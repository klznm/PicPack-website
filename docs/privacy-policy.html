Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2025

This privacy policy describes how RePack ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

RePack is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app operates entirely offline and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with us or third parties.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device:

  • Trip Information: Trip names, destinations, dates, and colors you create
  • Item Data: Item names and photos you add to your trips
  • Storage Items (Pro): Permanently saved items for reuse across trips
  • Templates (Pro): Custom trip templates you create
  • App Preferences: Theme settings (light/dark mode) and other app configurations

How We Use Your Information

All data is stored locally on your device and is used solely to:

  • Display your trips, items, and packing lists
  • Save your preferences and app settings
  • Provide Pro features if you purchase them

We do NOT:

  • Collect any personal information
  • Transmit your data to our servers or any third-party servers
  • Track your usage or behavior
  • Use analytics or advertising services
  • Require you to create an account
  • Access your data in any way

Android Auto Backup

RePack uses Android's Auto Backup feature to back up app data to your personal Google Drive account. This backup includes:

  • Trip and item data (database)
  • App preferences

Important Notes:

  • Backups are stored in your personal Google Drive, not on our servers
  • We have no access to your backup data
  • Photos may not be included in Auto Backup due to size limitations
  • You can disable Auto Backup in Android system settings if desired
  • Data is automatically restored when you reinstall the app on the same device or a new device

Photo Storage

Photos you add to items are stored in the app's private storage directory on your device. These photos:

  • Are NOT added to your device's photo gallery
  • Are NOT accessible to other apps
  • Are deleted when you uninstall the app
  • May exceed Android's Auto Backup size limit and may not be backed up automatically

In-App Purchases

If you purchase the Pro version of RePack:

  • Your purchase is processed by Google Play Store
  • We do not collect or store your payment information
  • Google Play may collect purchase data according to their privacy policy
  • Your Pro status is stored locally on your device

Third-Party Services

RePack uses the following third-party services provided by Google:

  • Google Play Billing: For processing in-app purchases (Pro version)
  • Google Play Core: For in-app review prompts

These services are subject to Google's privacy policies. We do not receive personal information from these services.

Children's Privacy

RePack does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app can be safely used by all ages.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data persists until you manually delete it or uninstall the app
  • You can delete individual trips or items at any time within the app
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all local data
  • Clearing app data in Android settings will permanently delete all local data

Security

Your data is protected by Android's built-in security features:

  • Data is stored in the app's private storage, inaccessible to other apps
  • No data is transmitted over the internet
  • Your device's security measures (encryption, lock screen) protect your data

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no data for us to access, modify, or delete. You have complete control over your data on your device:

  • You can delete trips and items at any time
  • You can export your data (future feature)
  • You can uninstall the app to permanently delete all data

Summary: RePack respects your privacy. Your data stays on your device. We don't collect, transmit, or share any personal information. You are in complete control of your data.
