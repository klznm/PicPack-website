Why PicPack?

Designed to make packing simple, visual, and stress-free.

📸

Visual Packing

Forget text-only lists. Snap photos of your actual items so you know exactly what you've packed and what's missing.

👆

Swipe to Check

Intuitive gestures make the process feel natural. Swipe right to pack, swipe left to skip. It's that easy.

🔒

100% Private & Offline

Your data lives on your phone. No accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking. Total privacy, guaranteed.

📦

Smart Storage

Save frequently used items to your "Storage" to quickly add them to future trips without re-taking photos.

📋

Templates

Create reusable templates for your common trips—like "Weekend Getaway" or "Gym"—to pack in seconds.

🎨

Beautiful Design

Built with Material Design 3, featuring dynamic colors and a clean, modern interface that looks great in light or dark mode.
