Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2, 2026

This privacy policy describes how PicPack ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

PicPack is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app operates entirely offline and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with us or third parties.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device:

  • Trip Information: Trip names, destinations, dates, and colors you create
  • Item Data: Item names and photos you add to your trips
  • Storage Items (Pro): Permanently saved items for reuse across trips
  • Templates (Pro): Custom trip templates you create
  • App Preferences: Theme settings (light/dark mode) and other app configurations

How We Use Your Information

All data is stored locally on your device and is used solely to:

  • Display your trips, items, and packing lists
  • Save your preferences and app settings
  • Provide Pro features if you purchase them

We do NOT:

  • Collect any personal information
  • Transmit your data to our servers or any third-party servers
  • Track your usage or behavior
  • Use analytics or advertising services
  • Require you to create an account
  • Access your data in any way

Android Auto Backup

PicPack uses Android's Auto Backup feature to back up app data to your personal Google Drive account. This backup includes:

  • Trip and item data (database)
  • App preferences

Important Notes:

  • Backups are stored in your personal Google Drive, not on our servers
  • We have no access to your backup data
  • Photos may not be included in Auto Backup due to size limitations
  • You can disable Auto Backup in Android system settings if desired
  • Data is automatically restored when you reinstall the app on the same device or a new device

Photo Storage

Photos you add to items are stored in the app's private storage directory on your device. These photos:

  • Are NOT added to your device's photo gallery
  • Are NOT accessible to other apps
  • Are deleted when you uninstall the app

In-App Purchases

If you purchase the Pro version of PicPack:

  • Your purchase is processed by Google Play Store
  • We do not collect or store your payment information
  • Google Play may collect purchase data according to their privacy policy

Contact Us

Email: klzn.dev@gmail.com

Summary: PicPack respects your privacy. Your data stays on your device. We don't collect, transmit, or share any personal information. You are in complete control of your data.
